## 6. Voice and writing style

- Clear, direct and technically accurate.
- Explain acronyms on first use for broad audiences.
- Prefer concrete examples over buzzwords.
- State trade-offs and limitations.
- Use first person where sharing personal experience.
- Avoid exaggerated claims, inflated adjectives and generic motivational writing.
- Separate professional experience from personal experimentation.
