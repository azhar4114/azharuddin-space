## Introduction

Event-driven integration has become the standard approach for connecting loosely coupled services in cloud environments. Rather than services calling each other directly, they publish events to a shared bus or queue and consumers react to those events independently.

This article covers common patterns that come up repeatedly in cloud integration work: fan-out, ordering, exactly-once semantics, dead-letter handling, and observability.
