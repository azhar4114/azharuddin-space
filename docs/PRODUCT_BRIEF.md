# Product Brief — azharuddin.space

## 1. Product definition

**Product name:** azharuddin.space  
**Product type:** Personal technology hub / professional portfolio / technical knowledge base  
**Owner:** Mohammad Azharuddin  
**Primary tagline:** Turning ideas into useful software.  
**Primary visual direction:** Dark developer aesthetic with professional minimalism.

azharuddin.space is the owner's public digital identity. It combines a professional portfolio, a technical knowledge hub and a showcase for independent software projects.

It is not intended to be a generic resume page, a full-service software agency website or a personal diary.

## 2. Audiences and their needs

### A. Recruiters and hiring managers
They need to understand within seconds:
- Who Azharuddin is.
- Years and areas of professional experience.
- Current technical focus.
- Relevant skills and role alignment.
- Career history and evidence of delivery.
- How to view/download the resume and make contact.

Design implication: concise hero, clear role positioning, scannable experience, skills, project evidence and prominent resume link.

### B. Developers and technology enthusiasts
They want:
- Practical engineering articles.
- Project case studies.
- Architecture explanations.
- Small useful browser tools.
- Lessons learned and implementation details.

Design implication: excellent reading experience, code formatting, tags, search/filtering and technically credible case studies.

### C. Potential clients
They want to know:
- What problems Azharuddin can help solve.
- What kinds of software and integrations he builds.
- Whether there is evidence of practical delivery.
- How to discuss a project.

Design implication: clear service descriptions, selected independent work, contact CTA and no inflated agency claims.

## 3. Product goals

1. Establish a credible, memorable professional identity.
2. Improve the quality of recruiter conversations and job opportunities.
3. Demonstrate engineering capability through projects and case studies.
4. Build a searchable body of technical writing.
5. Provide useful small developer tools that encourage repeat visits.
6. Create a channel for suitable consulting and freelance enquiries.
7. Keep ownership portable through a Git-based content workflow.

## 4. Success measures

Measure meaningful outcomes, not vanity traffic:
- Resume downloads.
- LinkedIn/GitHub outbound clicks.
- Visits to project case studies.
- Article reads and organic search visits.
- Usage of browser tools.
- Qualified contact enquiries.
- Recruiter conversations and interview invitations attributable to the site.

Do not add invasive tracking. Start with privacy-conscious analytics and review whether each metric is useful.

## 5. Product principles

- **Credibility over hype:** only publish claims that can be supported.
- **Useful over decorative:** every section should help one of the audiences.
- **Static-first:** pages should be fast, indexable and inexpensive to host.
- **Progressive enhancement:** core content works without client-side JavaScript.
- **Privacy by default:** browser tools process user input locally.
- **Maintainability:** content should be easy to update in Markdown or typed data files.
- **Portability:** avoid vendor lock-in and unnecessary services.

## 6. Version 1 scope

### Included
- Responsive homepage.
- About page.
- Experience timeline.
- Skills/technology index.
- Projects listing and case studies.
- Technical writing listing and article pages.
- Lab page with at least three browser-based tools.
- Services page.
- Contact page.
- Resume page/download link.
- SEO metadata, sitemap, robots.txt and social preview metadata.
- Git-based content management.
- Deployment configuration and documentation.

### Explicitly deferred
- User accounts and authentication.
- Database.
- Admin dashboard/CMS.
- AI chatbot.
- Newsletter subscription backend.
- Payments or subscription system.
- Complex contact-management CRM.
- Server-side processing of tool inputs.

## 7. Positioning

Suggested short positioning:
“Software engineer building enterprise software, cloud integrations, automation and independent digital products.”

Suggested longer positioning:
“I'm a software engineer with 13+ years of IT experience across enterprise applications, integrations, cloud engineering and automation. I enjoy solving complex engineering problems, building practical software and sharing what I learn.”

The 13+ years statement and all role-specific claims must be checked against the current approved resume before publication.

## 8. Primary calls to action

- Explore projects.
- View/download resume.
- Read technical writing.
- Try developer tools.
- Discuss a project.
- Connect on LinkedIn.

## 9. Content governance

Before publishing any personal or professional fact, verify:
- Employment dates and titles.
- Employer/client naming permissions.
- Certifications and education.
- Technical proficiency.
- Project ownership and public status.
- Any quantitative achievement.

Client work must be described at a level permitted by employment agreements and client confidentiality. Use synthetic examples and recreated diagrams.
